# HEALINK MAX30102

The MAX30102 provides the PPG path for heart-rate and SpO2 estimation.

## Current signal-quality logic

- 300-sample processing history at 100 Hz.
- HR is not trusted until a 2-second history is available.
- Peak detection uses a relative threshold over the observed IR range.
- Candidate beats obey the configured physiological interval bounds.
- At least two valid beat intervals are required before reporting HR.
- Optical DC presence gates prevent uncovered-sensor noise from being treated as a finger measurement.
- Invalid HR/SpO2 windows are cleared rather than exposing stale numeric output.
- Expected FIFO `EAGAIN` conditions do not become runtime sensor faults; freshness is handled by staleness logic.

These are engineering signal-quality rules, not clinical calibration.
Physical finger placement and stable contact are required for meaningful measurements.
